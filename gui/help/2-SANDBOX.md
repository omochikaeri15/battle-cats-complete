# Sandbox
You can emulate in-game battles and gameplay using the `Sandbox` page.

## Lineup
Within a lineup, you can add cats by using the cat list on the left, through either click or drag. The Bench is a store detached from the battle where you can put units you often swap between. Cats will show their level, cost, talent orbs, and whether they have any talents, as well as if the stages current restriction affects them.
